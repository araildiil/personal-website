Park Bo Gum

Actor & singer, born 1993

About Park Bo Gum

June 16, 1993 - present

Park Bo Gum is a South Korean actor and singer, born in Seoul, who built his reputation on warm, sincere performances rather than flashy roles. He made his screen debut in 2011, but it was a supporting turn as a gentle, unassuming friend in a hit 1980s-set ensemble drama that made him a household name. He went on to lead major romance and coming-of-age dramas, stepped away for mandatory military service, and returned to keep taking on both period dramas and modern stories.

Career highlights

  1. Film debut in 2011, followed by early supporting TV roles
  2. Breakout role in the 1980s-set ensemble drama Reply 1988 (2015)
  3. Lead role in the historical romance Love in the Moonlight (2016)
  4. Starred as an aspiring model in the coming-of-age drama Record of Youth (2020)
  5. Completed mandatory military service (2020-2022), then returned to acting
  6. Continued with film, drama, and hosting/variety work in the years after

What he's known for

  • A calm, sincere acting style that avoids melodrama
  • Strong chemistry with co-stars in romance dramas
  • Singing and songwriting alongside acting
  • Hosting variety and travel-style programs
  • A reputation for politeness and humility among fans and colleagues
